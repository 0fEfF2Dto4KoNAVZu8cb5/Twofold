Behavior and limits:
- Share-to-reveal is an optional shared interaction rule. Trusted server/admin
  and Immich access remain outside it. Blurred previews reveal coarse colors.
- Incoming turn reminder + at most one optional inexact follow-up around one hour.
  Android may delay work. No random daily prompt, exact alarm or battery benchmark.
- ntfy is optional per phone. It carries encrypted generic sync hints/challenges;
  photos and metadata travel directly over Twofold HTTPS. Periodic recovery stays.
- Database changes are additive at startup. Take a stopped-service backup/snapshot
  before upgrading. Retention remains latest sets plus pending Immich uploads;
  small receipt and round records remain for retry/unlock correctness.
- Raw originals/full metadata behavior is retained. GPS starts off. Actual camera
  fields depend on the hardware. Concurrent mode does not guarantee synchronized
  exposures. Missing or previously stripped metadata cannot be reconstructed.
- Both raw JPEGs stay separate; chosen main/inset layout is stored in JSON/XMP and
  used for the app/widget preview. Immich displays only supported metadata fields.
